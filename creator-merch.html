The process

Four steps. Only one is yours.

  • We talk. Your audience, your taste, your price level. We shape the brand around who follows you.
  • We build. Name, look, labels and a first small collection, ready for you to approve.
  • We make. We produce, stock and ship every order. We carry the stock, so you don't.
  • You promote. You do what you already do best. You earn on every piece that sells.
Questions

Asked backstage.

What is the difference between merch and a clothing brand?

Merch is usually a product line tied to your name. A brand has its own name, look and labels and can stand on its own. We build either, and many creators start with merch and grow into a brand.

Can you handle shipping and returns?

Yes. We store the stock and handle orders, shipping and returns.

Do I have to buy stock upfront?

We carry the stock. How earnings are shared is agreed with you in writing before anything is made.

Which products can you make?

Knit, woven and active clothing.
