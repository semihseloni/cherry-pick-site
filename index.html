Scene 01History

Twenty-seven years, no credits.

For 27 years we have made clothes for other people's brands. We design them, source them, produce them and get them delivered. Our names were never on the label, and that was the job.

Today we do the same work for the people who built the audience. We don't put ourselves in front. We put your brand out, and the whole show behind it is ours.

IntermissionQuestions

Asked backstage.

Do I need to know anything about making clothes?

No. You bring the audience and the taste. We handle design, sampling, labels, production and delivery, and we explain every step in plain words.

Who carries the stock and the risk?

We do. We produce, store and ship, so you are never sitting on boxes in a spare room. How earnings are shared is agreed with you in writing before anything is made.

Do I need a big following?

You need an engaged one. Followers who already ask what you wear matter more than a number. Tell us your size, country and age range and we will say honestly what we think.

Will my customers know Cherry Pick is behind it?

Only if you want them to. It is your brand, your name and your labels. We stay behind the doors.

How long does a first collection take?

It depends on the pieces and the season, so we plan backwards from your launch date. Most first collections are a small, focused drop rather than a whole range.

Where do you ship from and to?

We are active in the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada, and we plan stock and shipping around where your audience lives.

How do I start my own clothing brand as an influencer?

Start with your audience and your taste. Cherry Pick handles the rest of the brand development: name, logo, design, samples, labels, production, stock and shipping. You approve the pieces and promote them.

Do you do private label clothing and merch for creators?

Yes. We make private label clothing and creator merch under your name and your labels: knit, woven and active pieces, from a small first drop to repeat orders.

What is an influencer brand development agency?

An agency that turns an audience into a product brand. We build the brand and the product behind the scenes, and the creator stays in front as the face of it.
